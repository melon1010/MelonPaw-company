# Product

## Register

product

## Users

MelonPaw-Company serves system administrators, operations staff, project members, and employees inside one privately deployed enterprise. They use the console throughout the workday to operate personal Agents, manage shared resources, distribute skills, and inspect execution results.

## Product Purpose

The product gives one enterprise a controlled place to configure, run, and govern AI Agents. Success means that each role can quickly find the resources and actions it owns, while permissions, personal workspaces, approvals, and audit boundaries remain explicit.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, precise, and capable. The interface should feel like a dependable workbench: dense enough for repeated use, restrained in color, and direct in its language.

## Anti-references

- Large global headers that consume vertical space without adding task context.
- Marketing-style compositions, decorative cards, oversized headings, or visual effects that compete with operational work.
- Important actions hidden behind role-specific branches when the underlying permission already supports the workflow.
- One-note monochrome surfaces that make navigation, content, and state difficult to distinguish.

## Design Principles

- Keep the task surface primary; navigation and account controls stay available without taking over the content area.
- Expose complete workflows at the point of use, including empty states, conflicts, loading, and permission-aware actions.
- Use familiar controls and consistent action language so frequent operations require little relearning.
- Make ownership visible: personal resources, shared pools, projects, and administrative scope should never be ambiguous.
- Preserve security boundaries while removing setup and navigation friction that does not improve safety.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA for contrast and keyboard operation. Do not rely on color alone for state, retain visible focus treatment, support reduced motion, and keep navigation and action labels usable in all supported languages and responsive layouts.
